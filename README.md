### Profile

I am an AI researcher focused on building careful, reproducible methods in computer vision, graph-based learning, and generative modeling. I enjoy independently implementing scientific ideas from the ground up and evaluating what actually works under realistic conditions.

My work centers on representation learning, hierarchical graph models, foundation-model embeddings, and explainable systems. I have hands-on experience with large-scale satellite foundation models (**Galileo** and **AlphaEarth**) for crop classification and agricultural mapping, as well as classical and deep approaches to medical image analysis and biomedical graphs.

I am currently working on **Semantic 2D CAD-to-BIM Reconstruction** - using hierarchical graph learning and conditional flow matching to turn raw architectural drawings into structured, parametric building information models.

I am particularly interested in medical AI and trustworthy systems for clinical decision support. At the same time, I develop and apply methods across domains where geometry, structure, and uncertainty matter.

### Selected Research Projects

**Current Research**

- **Semantic 2D CAD-to-BIM Reconstruction of Architectural Models** *(Ongoing)*  
  Hierarchical graph learning and conditional flow matching for reconstructing parametric BIM models from raw 2D CAD drawings (drawing entities → architectural elements → spatial relations). Focus on semantic reconstruction, geometric consistency, and uncertainty-aware completion of missing parameters.

**Graph Learning & Biomedical**

- [**Sliced Wasserstein Graph Kernel for Brain Network Diagnosis**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/SWG-Kernel-Brain-Network-Diagnosis)  
  Independent reproduction of a MICCAI 2023 graph-kernel method using spectral graph representations, optimal transport geometry, and kernel-based classification.

- [**Graph Neural Networks for Metabolic Pathway Analysis**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/graph-neural-network-metabolic-pathway-analysis)  
  From-scratch Graph Convolutional Network for enzyme classification on KEGG biological networks.

- [**Retinal Vessel Segmentation using Mathematical Morphology**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/retinal-vessel-segmentation-using-mathematical-morphology)  
  Interpretable multi-scale vessel segmentation pipeline developed from first principles for medical image analysis.

**Computer Vision & Foundation Models**

- [**GeoAI Crop Mapping with Satellite Foundation Models**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/GeoAI-Crop-Mapping-Platform-with-Satellite-Foundation-Models)  
  Collaborative project using AlphaEarth and Galileo foundation-model embeddings for crop classification and vegetation analysis.

- [**Attention U-Net for Sentinel-2 Land-Cover Segmentation**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/attention-unet-sentinel2-landcover-segmentation)  
  Custom Attention U-Net for multispectral semantic segmentation (mIoU 0.926).

- [**Sentinel-2 Unsupervised Land-Cover Labeling**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/sentinel2-unsupervised-landcover-labeling)  
  Explainable unsupervised pseudo-labeling pipeline using Google Earth Engine and ensemble learning.

**Explainable & Classical Methods**

- [**Interpretable TSK Fuzzy Model for Asphalt Performance Prediction**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/interpretable-tsk-fuzzy-asphalt-model)  
  Transparent fuzzy inference system combining clustering, feature selection, and explainable prediction.

- [**BUSI Lesion XAI Critical Audit**](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/BUSI-Lesion-XAI-Critical-Audit)  
  Independent re-implementation and critical audit of a medical imaging XAI study, including feature consistency checks and SHAP/LIME analysis.

### Research Approach

I prioritize methodological understanding over black-box results.

- Independent reproduction of research methods  
- From-scratch implementation when official code is unavailable  
- Experimental design driven by clear questions  
- Careful analysis of assumptions, limitations, and failure modes  
- Fully reproducible computational pipelines  
- Transparent documentation of methods and results

I aim to move rigorously from:

**research question → method → implementation → experiment → evidence**
