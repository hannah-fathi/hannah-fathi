## 🧠 Selected Research Projects

### Medical AI & Biomedical Learning

**[Sliced Wasserstein Graph Kernel for Brain Network Diagnosis](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/SWG-Kernel-Brain-Network-Diagnosis)**
Independent reproduction of a **MICCAI 2023** graph-kernel method for brain-network classification, involving spectral graph representations, optimal-transport geometry, Sliced Wasserstein construction, and kernel-based learning.

**[Retinal Vessel Segmentation using Mathematical Morphology](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/retinal-vessel-segmentation-using-mathematical-morphology)**
An interpretable retinal-vessel segmentation pipeline implemented from first principles using multi-scale mathematical morphology and classical image-processing techniques.

**[Graph Neural Networks for Metabolic Pathway Analysis](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/graph-neural-network-metabolic-pathway-analysis)**
A from-scratch Graph Convolutional Network for metabolic-pathway analysis and enzyme classification using KEGG biological networks.

---

### Earth Observation & Computer Vision

**[GeoAI Crop Mapping with Satellite Foundation Models](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/GeoAI-Crop-Mapping-Platform-with-Satellite-Foundation-Models)**
An end-to-end GeoAI project exploring satellite foundation-model representations, including **AlphaEarth and Galileo embeddings**, for agricultural mapping and vegetation analysis.

**[Attention U-Net for Sentinel-2 Land-Cover Segmentation](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/attention-unet-sentinel2-landcover-segmentation)**
A multispectral semantic-segmentation pipeline using a custom Attention U-Net and spectral indices for agricultural land-cover analysis.

**Reported mIoU: 0.926 on the evaluated experimental setup.**

**[Sentinel-2 Unsupervised Land-Cover Labeling](https://github.com/hannah-fathi/sentinel2-unsupervised-landcover-labeling)**
An explainable unsupervised pseudo-labeling pipeline combining spectral features, Google Earth Engine, clustering, and ensemble learning.

## 🔎 Research Approach

I am most interested in the point where a research method has to move from **paper to implementation and from implementation to evidence**.

I enjoy:

* Reading research methods closely enough to reproduce them independently
* Implementing algorithms from scratch when official implementations are unavailable
* Designing experiments around clear methodological questions
* Examining assumptions, representations, limitations, and failure modes
* Building reproducible computational pipelines
* Documenting methods and results so that they can be inspected and reproduced

I aim to develop as a researcher who can move carefully from:

**research question → method → implementation → experiment → evidence**

rather than treating models as black boxes.
